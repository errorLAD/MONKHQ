Under Meta Platform Terms and global data protection regulations (GDPR/CCPA), users have the right to request deletion of any personal data associated with their Instagram or Facebook accounts.

How to Request Data Deletion:

  1. Go to your Facebook / Instagram account's Settings & Privacy.
  2. Click Apps and Websites and find MonkHQ.
  3. Click Remove and check the option to request deletion of data.
  4. Meta will automatically trigger this endpoint and send you a confirmation link with your tracking code.
